Built to be boring
about the scary parts

Everything a debloat flow should do by default — and most tools skip.

01No root. Ever.

Only adb shell pm … --user 0 — stock Android mechanism. Knox, bootloader, warranty and Play Integrity are never touched.

02Preview or nothing

You always see the full removal list first and must type sim. There is no flag that skips the preview.

03Reversible by design

Every action is logged. Menu option 4 restores each app; tweaks --reset returns every setting to its original value.

04Presets that explain

Every one of the 51–126 packages has a plain-language line: what it is, and what stops working without it.

05Speaks your language

Menus, warnings and errors follow your OS — Português, English, Español. The whole flow, not just the docs.

06Tested, not on your phone

73 checks run against a fake adb simulating One UI 9. Your firmware is the final preview — which is why the preview exists.

How a run actually goes

Five steps. Nothing happens without step 3.

01

Diagnostics

The tool checks adb, the device and your Android/One UI version before anything else.

02

Preview

Only packages that actually exist on your phone are listed — minus a built-in deny-list of critical components. Each line explains itself.

03

Typed confirmation

sim or nothing happens. Closing the window cancels safely.

04

Apply

pm uninstall --user 0 per package; failures fall back to disabling the app with its data preserved.

05

Undo anytime

Menu option 4 restores everything this tool ever removed. Reboot, done.

Why not Canta or UAD?

Universal Android Debloater and Canta are great — scamsung-debloat borrows risk knowledge from them. It exists for the people they don't serve well: Galaxy owners who don't want to learn a new app or keep Shizuku alive, who want a menu in their own language, presets built specifically for One UI 8/9 (from itxjobe's community-tested lists), and a restore path that is one menu option, not a factory reset.

Quick answers

Does it need root? Does it trip Knox?+

No and no. It uses adb shell pm … --user 0, a stock Android feature. Bootloader, /system, Knox and Play Integrity stay untouched — banking apps and Google Pay keep working.

Can I undo everything?+

Yes — menu option 4 restores every app the tool removed, and tweaks --reset returns each setting to its recorded original value.

Will an OTA update bring the apps back?+

Samsung OTAs sometimes reinstall their apps. Nothing breaks — run the same preset again after the update.

Does it free up storage?+

No. APKs stay in the read-only system partition. The gains are RAM, background battery drain, privacy and a clean app drawer — not gigabytes.

What if an app I keep depends on a removed one?+

That feature stops working (e.g. watch faces need Samsung services). Every preset line warns you, the preview shows the full list first, and restore brings the dependency back.

Ready when you are.

adb installed, USB debugging on, one double-click. The preview shows up before your phone changes in any way.
