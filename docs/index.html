Undo is a command.

restore walks the state file and reinstalls each app through install-existing. Tweaks go back to their recorded values. The record lives on your computer and survives reboots, so the way back works next week or next year.

Good questions.

Does it need root? Does it trip Knox?

No and no. Package removal with --user 0 is a stock Android feature. Knox, the bootloader and Play Integrity never notice. Banking apps and Google Pay keep working.

Can I undo everything?

Yes. restore reinstalls each app through install-existing and returns tweaks to their recorded values. The state file lives on your computer and survives reboots.

Will an OTA bring the apps back?

Sometimes Samsung reinstalls its apps after an update. Nothing breaks; run the same preset again and carry on.

Does it free up storage?

Not gigabytes. APKs live in a read-only partition. What you get back is RAM, background battery and an app drawer that stops being a billboard.

What if an app I keep depends on a removed one?

That feature breaks until restore: watch faces, pay plugins, Galaxy Store items. Every preset line warns you first, the preview shows the final list, and restore brings the dependency back.
